About Us

Founded in 2005, Onspec Precision Machining has been providing exceptional machining services to clients throughout the region. Our team of highly skilled machinists and technicians combine years of experience with cutting-edge technology to deliver precise, reliable results.

We pride ourselves on our commitment to quality, accuracy, and customer satisfaction. Whether you need custom parts for prototyping or large-scale production runs, we have the expertise and equipment to meet your needs.

Our state-of-the-art facility is equipped with CNC machines, lathes, mills, and other precision tools that allow us to tackle even the most complex machining projects with ease.
